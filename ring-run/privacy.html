Ring Run Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Ring Run is a game for iPhone and Android. This page explains what information is used when you play it.

The game itself

The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal details. Your best score is saved only on your own phone. It never leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

Ring Run is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the top of the screen and a full-screen ad now and then between games. To show and measure these ads, Google may collect information from your phone, such as its advertising ID, IP address, a rough location based on that address, and how you interact with the ads. Google uses this information as described in its policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the game asks for your choice before showing personalised ads. You can change that choice at any time with the Privacy choices link on the game's start screen. On iPhone, the game asks for permission before ads may use your phone's advertising ID, and you can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Ring Run is meant for players aged 13 and over. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact details on Ring Run's page in Google Play or the App Store.
