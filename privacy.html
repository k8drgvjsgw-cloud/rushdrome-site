Rushdrome

Privacy Policy · Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: we (the makers of Razo) do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. There are no accounts. The Android and iPhone apps show ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show those ads (see below). The website version and the console and computer apps have no ads.

What the game keeps

The game saves your progress (credits, cars you own, colours and upgrades, best times, sound and camera settings) on your own device only. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

What we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or other contact details.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No accounts and no in-app purchases.
  • We receive no information about you from the ads service.

Ads in the Android and iPhone apps

The Android and iPhone apps are free and show a full-screen ad from Google AdMob after some finished races, never during a race. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone only if you allow it when asked),
  • your IP address and the approximate location it suggests,
  • information about the ads shown and how you interact with them,
  • basic device and app information and crash data.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. Where the law asks (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the app asks for your consent or lets you opt out before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the home screen. On iPhone you can turn tracking off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads). If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

Console and computer apps

These apps run fully offline and show no ads. They do not connect to the internet while you play. Controllers are read on your device only to steer and to move through menus.

The game itself plays offline in every app. The Android and iPhone apps use the internet only to load ads.

The website version

The website version has no ads. It is delivered by our web hosting provider, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit) for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to identify you. The game itself stores your progress in your browser's storage on your device.

Deleting your data

Because your progress is kept only on your device, you can remove it at any time by deleting the app, or on the website by clearing the site's data in your browser settings. We hold no data about you to delete. For information Google holds for ads, use the choices above or Google's own tools at myadcenter.google.com.

Children

Razo is suitable for all ages. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The phone apps ask Google to show only ads suitable for a general audience (rated PG or lower). The apps are not directed at children under 13.

Open-source software

The game's code, cars, track, music and sound effects are original. It uses the three.js 3D library (MIT License) and the Chakra Petch and Saira Condensed fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1). The Android and iPhone apps are built with Flutter and use open-source Flutter packages and the Google Mobile Ads SDK under their own licences. The full notices are included with the game.

Changes to this policy
